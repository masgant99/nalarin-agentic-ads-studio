Agentic Ads Studio

Agentic Ads Studio is a private paid media operations platform operated by your organization for authorized agency staff managing client advertising accounts.

Purpose of the application

The application connects Google Ads, Meta Ads, and TikTok Ads accounts after explicit provider authorization. It lets operators choose an authorized ad account, review campaign and ad performance, prepare reports, and manage campaigns through human-reviewed workflows.

Google Ads features

  • Read campaign and ad performance through the official Google Ads API.
  • Create Google Search campaigns in PAUSED state.
  • Pause or enable campaigns only after a human confirmation.
  • Add negative keywords after an operator preview and confirmation.

Human-controlled operation

The platform never starts advertising spend silently. New campaigns are created paused, every write action requires explicit confirmation, and the dedicated reporting bot has read-only access.
