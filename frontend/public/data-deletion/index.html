Agentic Ads Studio Data Deletion

Users may remove their Meta Ads connection and request deletion of associated data at any time.

Disconnect inside the application

  1. Sign in at your-domain.example.
  2. Open Facebook Campaigns.
  3. Select Disconnect in the Meta Ads connection panel.
  4. Revoke Agentic Ads Studio in Facebook Settings under Business Integrations if you also want to revoke the provider grant.

Request complete deletion

Email privacy@your-domain.example from the address associated with your account. Include the Meta ad account ID to identify the connection. After identity and authorization are verified, encrypted OAuth credentials and stored provider connection records are deleted within 30 days.

What is deleted

The deletion covers stored Meta OAuth credentials, ad account identifiers, and connection metadata controlled by Agentic Ads Studio. Data retained independently by Meta remains subject to Meta's own policies.
